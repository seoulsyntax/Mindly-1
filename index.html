🚨 Urgent Support

If you feel that you or someone else is in immediate danger:


1. Move to a safe place.
2. Stay with a trusted person.
3. Tell a trusted adult, counsellor, doctor or mental-health professional.
4. Contact the appropriate verified local emergency/crisis service.

🎯 Our Purpose

MINDLY is designed as a mental-wellness support platform that helps users self-check their wellbeing, understand areas that may need attention and find appropriate next steps.

🔄 Our Approach

CHECK
Reflect on your wellbeing.

UNDERSTAND
Make sense of your responses.

GUIDE
Find suitable next steps.

CONNECT
Reach appropriate human support.

🔒 Privacy

Privacy is a core design principle. The production system can use secure authentication, controlled data access and privacy-conscious storage.

⚠️ Important

This prototype is not intended to diagnose, treat or replace professional mental-health care.
